# Product Direction

## What this site is

A recruiter-facing career portfolio for Sanjeyan Chrysharnthan, a Computer Engineering student at NTU building applied machine-learning systems, quantitative research infrastructure, and production software.

The portfolio is not a gallery of every repository. It is a concise, evidence-backed argument that Sanjeyan can take an ambiguous technical problem, build a credible system, and verify the result.

## Audience

Primary audiences are internship recruiters, hiring managers, and engineers evaluating Sanjeyan for 2027 roles in applied ML, quantitative development, and software systems.

They should be able to answer three questions within one minute:

1. What kind of engineer is he becoming?
2. What are the strongest examples of his work?
3. Which claims are verified, and where are the boundaries?

## Core narrative

**Computer Engineering @ NTU · Applied ML Systems, Quantitative Research & Production Software.**

The default journey is direct and editorial: career thesis, proof, technical experience, three flagship case studies, a smaller evidence archive, focused capabilities, and a precise contact close. Visitors never need to choose a track before seeing the work.

## Content rules

- Prefer three deep, current case studies over a uniform grid of projects.
- Attach numbers only when they have an evidence boundary.
- State synthetic-data, team-attribution, and private-work constraints plainly.
- Keep weak, blocked, planning-only, or insufficiently attributable work out of the public default view.
- Treat work case studies, personal repositories, OSS contributions, competitions, and team projects as distinct evidence types.
- Preserve employment dates exactly.

## Flagship evidence

1. Algorithmic Trading Backtester: public repository, 712 tests, Ruff and strict mypy, reproducible data and analytics architecture.
2. Market Risk Forecasting Lab: public repository, 204 tests, multi-model risk system with an explicit synthetic-only empirical boundary.
3. Panasonic SAM3 Systems Work: a case study covering interactive video segmentation, GPU serving reliability, SSE previews, and selection as one of four AI technologies for Panasonic AIMX Singapore × TechInnovation 2026; do not imply Sanjeyan was the public presenter.

## Visual direction

Retain the established editorial identity: Newsreader and IBM Plex, warm black/ivory, muted gold and green, sharp geometry, and the restrained candlestick motif. The signature interaction is an **evidence ledger**: expandable case-study details expose the problem, engineering decision, verification, and honest boundary.

Avoid generic dashboard cards, decorative technology tickers, glossy gradients, glass surfaces, shadows, oversized pill controls, and effect-heavy interactions. Motion should clarify hierarchy and state, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Success criteria

- The homepage renders immediately with one `h1`, a semantic `main`, and a `footer`.
- “Selected” is the default work view; Quant Systems, Applied ML, Product Systems, and Archive are optional inline filters.
- The three flagship case studies are visually and structurally distinct from the compact archive.
- Experience foregrounds Vertex Holdings, Panasonic R&D Centre Singapore, and Rohde & Schwarz Asia.
- Skills are capped at 12–15 evidence-backed capabilities; leadership foregrounds Hall 10 Financial Controller and NTU ODAC.
- Links have descriptive accessible names and touch targets are at least 44×44 px.
- Canonical, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata are present.
